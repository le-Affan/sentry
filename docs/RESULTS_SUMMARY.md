# Results Summary

*Last updated 2026-08-27. Paper-ready extract of [EVAL_RESULTS.md](EVAL_RESULTS.md).*

`Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct` base versus the same model with a 4-bit QLoRA adapter, on a
held-out test split of 40 incidents. Greedy decoding, fixed seed, identical system
prompt — the two columns differ only by the adapter.

---

## Training

QLoRA, r=16, α=32, dropout 0.05, on attention and MLP projections. LR 2e-4, cosine
schedule, 3 epochs, effective batch 8. Tesla T4 (Kaggle), 63 optimiser steps, 258.3
minutes. 360 training examples, 36 held out of train for eval loss.

| Epoch | Train loss | Eval loss |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.5838 | 1.3993 |
| 2 | 1.1469 | 1.1387 |
| 3 | **0.9748** | **1.1024** |

Loss decreased monotonically on train with no NaN or divergence; gradient norms held near
1.0 throughout. Eval improvement flattened at epoch 3 (+0.036) against continued train
improvement (+0.17), indicating the onset of overfitting on 324 effective examples.

## Interpretation

**Fine-tuning bought format and technique compliance, not analytical ability.** The two
largest movements are format adherence (30.0% → 100.0%) and technique-ID accuracy
(2.5% → 90.0%), and both share one cause: the base model had never seen this report
contract, so it invented its own structure — 28 of its 40 reports inject `###`
sub-headings inside the required sections — and guessed technique IDs essentially at
random, landing on the deprecated `T1086` seventeen times. The adapter taught it the
contract. ROUGE-L more than doubled and BERTScore rose from 0.8150 to 0.9102, consistent
with the same cause: tuned outputs are shorter (median 381 → 324 tokens) and stop
hedging, so they overlap the reference far more closely. Severity rose 47.5% → 82.5%, but
the base model scored *below* its own majority baseline of 66.0%, under-calling
`Critical` as `High` twelve times; the tuned model's +16.5 points over baseline is real
but far more modest than the technique figure suggests. **Grounding did not improve and
slightly regressed** — both variants leave 95.0% of reports free of invented entities,
and the tuned model's ungrounded entity rate rose 2.1% → 3.4% because it names more
entities per report. Fine-tuning on grounded references did not teach grounding; the base
model was already mostly grounded and the headroom was too small to learn from.

**Two limitations govern how these numbers may be read.** First, the synthetic-telemetry
loop: every blob was produced by this project's own generator, and a bag of `event_type`
n-grams recovers the technique 89.8% of the time from event structure alone against a
17.4% baseline. The 90.0% technique accuracy therefore cannot be separated from that
structural leak and must not be read as a detection result. Second, the teacher-model
ceiling: all reference reports were generated by `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` against a
hand-curated 21-row technique lookup, so the tuned model is scored on agreement with a
teacher and a labelling table of this project's own construction; its ceiling is their
quality, and systematic errors in either are invisible to every metric above. The full
set is in [LIMITATIONS.md](LIMITATIONS.md).
